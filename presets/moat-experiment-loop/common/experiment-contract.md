# Experiment contract

Select exactly one latest valid `<!-- moat-experiment:current -->` packet from the issue/run PR. It must bind hypothesis, environment identity, baseline, ordered commands, acceptance observations, passed/failed/inconclusive branches, evidence paths, cleanup obligations, IaC children, and owning design location. A packet is stale or contradictory only when a later valid packet explicitly supersedes it, or live facts contradict one of the business bindings above. The run's own expected progress — run-branch commits, phase handoffs and evidence on the packet's ordered path, engine phase advancement, and declared lifecycle transitions of linked children — never invalidates the packet; no producer, gate, or reviewer may treat run-head, phase, or child-state advancement as staleness evidence, and producers never edit or republish the packet to track such progress. Missing, ambiguous, stale, or contradictory packets return to contract enrichment; downstream phases never silently repair them.
